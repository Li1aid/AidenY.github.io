### 4.1 CoLab — Hybrid Learning Platform
- **Category:** Education Platform · 2025
- **Role:** UI/UX · Frontend
- **Type:** Hybrid Learning
- **Tools:** Figma, Web
- **Tagline:** "A learning experience as refreshing as a sip of Coke."

**What it is:** A hybrid learning platform built for international design students. It pairs standardized online lectures and quizzes with an in-person tutorial space — letting students freely form skill-based teams, follow lectures at their own pace, and ask questions in real time through an embedded AI assistant and live chat.

**Key features:**
1. **Free Team-Up** — Skill-based profiles let students find compatible teammates in the same course; no random groups.
2. **Standardized Online Lectures** — Lecture content delivered online with quiz-verified attendance; students pace themselves, replay sections, and reduce language load.
3. **Quiz-verified Attendance** — Lecture quizzes confirm participation while giving immediate feedback on comprehension.
4. **In-class Tutorial Whiteboard** — During tutorials every student sees richer content on their own screen, hovers terms for AI explanations, and joins a live chat without interrupting class.

**Interactive demos on the project page:** Team Up matching, online lecture player (Grid Systems in Interaction Design), lecture quiz (3 questions), tutorial whiteboard with instructor Dr. Hannah Lee (1,247 viewers, 847 in chat), AI explanations for terms like kerning, baseline, negative space, hierarchy.

### 4.3 Anno — Companion Health Robot
- **Category:** Health Tech · 2024
- **Role:** Product · UX
- **Type:** Companion Robot
- **Tools:** Figma, Rhino, Web
- **Tagline:** "A companion-shaped health system for older adults living alone."

**What it is:** A puppy-shaped smart health companion designed for older adults living alone. It blends quiet, ambient health monitoring with the warmth of a pet — using voice, gentle body language, and a small side screen instead of apps and notifications. Medication reminders, anomaly detection, SOS protocols, and emotional feedback are wrapped in a form that feels like company, not a medical device.

**Key features:**
1. **Voice-Guided Conversation** — Natural-voice setup of medication reminders, health checks, alarms; no apps or smartphones, suitable for low digital literacy.
2. **Health Monitoring + SOS** — Built-in sensors and a wearable wristband passively monitor heart rate, temperature, and activity; anomalies trigger a voice prompt or automatic SOS.
3. **Smart Medication Dispenser** — Internal dispenser prepares pills at scheduled times; feedback via voice and subtle body movement; supports GP prescription uploads.
4. **Emotion-Based Companion** — Anno wags its tail, tilts its head, or moves slowly based on health state; in critical moments an animated screen shows heart-rate status.
5. **Privacy-Respecting Display** — Side screen shows daily summaries to the user; doctors and family receive only critical alerts.
6. **Senior-Friendly Form** — Visible, easy-press buttons; magnetic wristband docking; soft coverings hiding tech components.

**Demo content:** 5 scenes (Medication, Anomaly + SOS, Dispenser, Emotion, Privacy), Anno robot SVG (side and front views), health display (BPM, blood pressure), interactive panels and voice input simulation.

### 4.4 Whisper Field — Immersive Space for Neurodiversity
- **Category:** Immersive Experience · 2024
- **Role:** Spatial · Interaction
- **Type:** Installation · Public Light
- **Tools:** Unreal Engine 5, Processing, Figma
- **Tagline:** "A non-intrusive, multi-sensory space for ADHD and broader neurodiversity."

**What it is:** A two-part immersive system for people with ADHD and broader neurodiversity. Inside the **Cube**, four screens and a tactile grass floor co-create AI-generated landscapes that breathe through generation, presentation, and deconstruction. Outside, a **Catenary** canopy of flower-shaped lights blooms gently as people gather beneath. Both parts share one principle — **guiding, never dominating** — using delayed responses, low contrast, and per-person interaction zones to support free exploration without overstimulation.

**Cube features:**
- Three breathing phases: Generation → Presentation → Deconstruction
- Panoramic 4-wall screens with AI-generated landscapes
- Tactile grass floor interaction
- Phase HUD, landscape library selector
- Grass response controls: Delay (0–300 ms), Glow radius (5–40 cm), Auto-breathe cycle

**Catenary features:**
- Visitor counter / bloom meter (bloom % grows with crowd)
- Four light modes: Rest, Rainbow Butterfly, Awareness Ribbon, Paint Your Color (RGB custom)
- Flower-shaped lights on catenary wire structure
- Phone mockup for mode selection + QR code
