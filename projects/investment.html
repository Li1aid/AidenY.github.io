WHAT IT DOES

What it does.

A private dashboard for tracking multi-market holdings — and seeing whether my decisions agree with the AI's.

01

Cross-market holdings

A-shares, ASX, HK, mainland funds, physical gold — five asset classes, four currencies, one screen.

02

Daily AI advice

One button packages my holdings, indicators (MA, RSI) and filtered news; Claude returns a position-by-position recommendation.

03

Action log + alignment

Every action I take is auto-classified against that day's AI advice: agreed, opposed, partial. Did I beat the model? Now I can tell.

04

Equity curve + calendar

Nightly snapshots build the equity curve. A daily P&L job paints each day green or red on a calendar — the shape of a month at a glance.
