OVERVIEW

What is vividXperience?

vividXperience is a Vision Pro application that lets users experience the vibrant atmosphere of Vivid Sydney from home. Through immersive AR/VR, users explore digital replicas of light installations, navigate a 3D panoramic map of the festival, and interact with key highlights — making the festival accessible to anyone who can't attend in person.
