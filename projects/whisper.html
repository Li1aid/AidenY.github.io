OVERVIEW

What is Whisper Field?

Whisper Field is a two-part immersive system for individuals with ADHD and broader neurodiversity. Inside the Cube, four screens and a tactile grass floor co-create AI-generated landscapes that breathe through generation, presentation and deconstruction. Outside, a Catenary canopy of flower-shaped lights blooms gently as people gather beneath. Both parts share one principle — guiding, never dominating — using delayed responses, low contrast and per-person interaction zones to support free exploration without overstimulation.

STORYBOARD · CUBE

A walk through Whisper Field

01

Generation Phase

Screens start black. Particles slowly form. The grass tips glow with soft blue light.

02

Approach

As visitors move, particles around them respond — brightness, drift direction and motion patterns shift in resonance.

03

Co-creation

Lightly touching the wall summons new particles in that interaction zone — and the scene in that region updates accordingly.

04

Landscape Emerges

After interaction during generation, the resulting landscape gradually emerges — never quite the same twice.

05

Stable Scene

Once the scene is fully generated, interaction triggers secondary elements — birds, wind across grass, drifting clouds.

06

Shared Solitude

Multiple people immerse simultaneously — some interact, others sit still. Interaction zones stay within 15 cm, so privacy holds.

DESIGN PROCESS · CATENARY

Three iterations

VERSION 1

Wandering Light, Blooming Night

  • Nature themes and meditation
  • Flowers gradually bloom as the audience approaches
  • Color-blind friendly palette
REASON FOR ITERATION

Research revealed colour blindness is more a perceptual "mismatch" than a medical condition — so the target audience was reframed.

VERSION 2

Infinite Rainbow

  • Inspired by the neurodivergent infinity symbol
  • Focuses on dazzling lighting and smooth transitions
REASON FOR ITERATION

Spatial boundaries felt too strong. The centralised focal point limited engagement with the rest of the space.

FINAL VERSION

Mind in Bloom

  • Blooming flowers as a metaphor for neurodiversity
  • Shifts the theme toward understanding and empowerment for ADHD
  • Figma prototype + QR-driven interactive light modes
WHY IT LANDED

Combines the calming effect of nature with neurodivergent symbolism — public-facing, educational, and gentle.
